Documentation for Yo-spaces
Description
Yo-spaces is a community communication platform for organizations working with rural communities in Uganda.
-it helps them create groups that help them to pass communication to all members simultaneously especially those with basic feature phones through SMS, voice and USSD. 
-it saves time, information is passed on quickly and efficiently and feedback picked instantly.
Mission
-To connect rural communities through technology.
Vision
-To make communication in rural communities in Uganda easier and accessible
Tagline.
-making access to information in rural communities in Uganda easier through group voice conferencing, SMS and USSD.
Create a group via USSD, add members, Broadcast messages to all members.
The problem 
Uganda’s internet penetration remains low at 28% and with 24.7 million features phones and 13.3 million basic phones many users in rural areas delay to receive important information, medicine gets spoilt on shelves, Access to loans is delayed. Disrupted operations
